  build such as the AppImage) is allowed by all of the licenses above, as long
  as this attribution and these license notices go with them.
- **CC BY-NC-SA 3.0 shaders may not be used for commercial purposes**, and
  modified versions of them must be shared under the same license. A

  code (everything outside this directory) is MIT and has no such restriction.
- Images or videos rendered from these shaders are subject to the shaders'
  licenses too.

If you are one of the authors and would like your shader credited differently
